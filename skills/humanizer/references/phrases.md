# Phrases to watch

Fix when they cluster or when the sentence is empty without them. Prefer cut or restructure over synonym swap.

Sources: Kobak 2025, Wikipedia AI Cleanup, WriteHuman 2026, [stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop).

---

## Throat-clearing openers

State the content directly. Cut the announcement.

- Here's the thing / Here's what / Here's this / Here's that / Here's why
- The uncomfortable truth is / It turns out / The real X is
- Let me be clear / The truth is / I'll say it again
- I'm going to be honest / Can we talk about
- Here's what I find interesting / Here's the problem though
- Great question / Let's unpack / Let me break this down
- In today's fast-paced... / In recent years... / This paper introduces...

Any "here's what/this/that" construction is runway before the point.

---

## Emphasis crutches

Delete. They add no meaning.

- Full stop. / Period. / Let that sink in.
- This matters because / Make no mistake / Here's why that matters
- At its core / At the end of the day / When it comes to / In a world where / The reality is
- It's worth noting / It's important to note / Needless to say
- furthermore / moreover / additionally / Overall, / In conclusion, (on short pieces)

## High-signal vocabulary (Kobak 2025, Wikipedia)

delve, underscore, showcasing, tapestry, landscape, realm, navigate, leverage, utilize, facilitate, foster, bolster, align with, underpin, garner, highlight (empty verb), emphasizing, enhancing, boasts, testament, crucial, paramount, pivotal, intricate, meticulous, interplay, multifaceted, nuanced, robust, seamless, comprehensive, transformative, cutting-edge, vibrant, speaks volumes, paradigm shift, move the needle

**Prestige nouns often bundled:** ecosystem, symphony, labyrinth, beacon, cornerstone, bedrock, odyssey, mosaic, kaleidoscope

**Model drift:** 2023–24 leaned delve/tapestry/underscore; 2024–25 showcasing/fostering/emphasizing; 2025+ emphasizing/showcasing plus notability puffery.

## 2026 structural padding (WriteHuman)

ensuring, ensures, highlights, supports, reflects; significantly, effectively, increasingly (without a number); capable of → able to; stacked "rather than"

**Copula inflation:** serves as, stands as, functions as, features, boasts, offers → is / has / does. "is designed to" → does, helps, or drop.

## Meta-commentary

The piece should move, not announce its own structure.

- Hint: / Plot twist: / Spoiler:
- You already know this, but / But that's another post
- X is a feature, not a bug / Dressed up as
- The rest of this essay explains... / Let me walk you through...
- In this section, we'll... / As we'll see... / I want to explore...
- On one hand... on the other... (when the writer already picked a side)

## Performative emphasis and telling

- creeps in / I promise / They exist, I promise
- This is genuinely hard / This is what leadership actually looks like / actually matters
- Honestly, / Here's the thing (fake casual)
- So yeah / Pretty wild (bad humanizer pass)

---

## Vague declaratives

Announce importance without naming the thing. Cut or replace with the specific thing.

- The reasons are structural / The implications are significant
- This is the deepest problem / The stakes are high / The consequences are real
- pivotal moment, testament to, reflects broader trends, continues to captivate, leaves a lasting impact, solidifies its status, generated debate

Say who did what, when, with what proof.

---

## Adverbs and softeners

**Default:** cut filler adverbs that only add emphasis or fake sincerity.

- really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually
- deeply, truly, fundamentally, inherently, inevitably
- interestingly, importantly, crucially

**Keep** load-bearing hedges in academic, medical, legal, or financial text ("community-reported", "in our tests", real uncertainty). Genre beats blanket adverb bans.
