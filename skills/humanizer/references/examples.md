# Before/after examples

Plain after-lines, not polished copy. Meaning and facts preserved.

---

## Throat-clearing + binary contrast

**Before:** Here's the thing: building products is hard. Not because the technology is complex. Because people are complex. Let that sink in.

**After:** Building products is hard. Technology is manageable. People aren't.

## Filler + unnecessary reassurance

**After:** Teams struggle with alignment. Nobody admits confusion.

**After:** Move faster. Your competition is.

## Rhetorical setup

**Before:** What if I told you that the best teams don't optimize for productivity? Here's what I mean: they optimize for learning. Think about it.

**After:** The best teams optimize for learning, not productivity.

## False agency + narrator distance

**Before:** The decision emerges when the data tells us the market rewards speed. Nobody designed this workflow. The culture shifts on its own.

**After:** Someone reads the numbers and kills slow projects. You inherit the workflow. People change how they work when incentives change.

## Onboarding puffery (humanizer)

**Before:** In today's fast-paced digital landscape, it is worth noting that effective onboarding plays a pivotal role in user retention. Furthermore, a seamless experience can foster engagement and showcase the platform's robust capabilities.

**After:** If the first screen confuses people they leave. Fix the first-run flow before anything else. One clear goal, no blocking tour.

## Bot reply

**Before:** Great question! Check the network tab. Additionally, it's worth noting that CORS errors often manifest as generic fetch failures. Let me know if you'd like help!

**After:** Check the network tab first. CORS often looks like a generic fetch error. If that's clean, check `API_URL` in env. Staging is usually wrong.

## PR description

**Before:** This PR introduces a comprehensive refactoring, leveraging a modular architecture to facilitate maintainability and underscore our commitment to robust solutions.

**After:** Split evidence rendering into summary, trace, and chart modules. Same HTML and routes. Easier to change teaching display without rewiring the page.
