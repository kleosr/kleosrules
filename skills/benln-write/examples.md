# Examples

## Inbound sales reply (NiubiStar → vouch + trial)

**Input:** Reply to Enterprise Outreach pitch about `cursorkleosr`. Cursor ambassador. Want vouch + free trial on all projects. Meet in the middle. ProtonMail markdown. Sign off Mario.

**Output:**
```markdown
hey — saw the note on cursorkleosr.

i'm a cursor ambassador with a few projects in this space.
open to trying enterprise outreach if we meet in the middle: vouch + free trial across all of them.

deal?

best,
Mario
```

## Job-style cold email (guide format)

**Input:** Engineer emailing a seed-stage founder after a raise. Built relevant feature + side project with traction. Want a chat.

**Output:**
```markdown
hey kevin — saw you just raised seed and are hiring to ship product.

i built [feature] at [company] (big driver of arr) and run [project] (~100k active users).
think i could help you ship [product] faster — worth a chat?
```

## Bad → fix

**Bad:**
> Hi there, I hope this email finds you well. I came across your company and was truly impressed by your innovative mission. I'd love to pick your brain sometime and explore potential synergies. Let me know if you're open to connecting!

**Fixed:**
```markdown
hey — noticed you're hiring for [role] after the seed.

i shipped [concrete thing] at [company]; happy to send two relevant links if useful.
want those, or not a fit right now?
```
