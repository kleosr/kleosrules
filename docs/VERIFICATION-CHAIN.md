## Structural lesson

Local verifiable ≠ globally correct. Finite gauntlet ≠ decidability
([`MECHANICAL-INCOMPLETENESS.md`](MECHANICAL-INCOMPLETENESS.md)).
Mature practice **names** the gap, attacks relations, then closes with
production. Distrust any document — including ours — that claims a
“total unconditional” or “zero margin of error” advance past contact with
reality or past Rice-hard semantics.
Prompt-alone perfection is also impossible
([`evals/DETERMINISTIC-PLASTIC-PSTAR.md`](evals/DETERMINISTIC-PLASTIC-PSTAR.md));
required stack: persuasion + force + epistemic memory
([`AGENTIAL-CONTROL.md`](AGENTIAL-CONTROL.md)).

**V11 ceiling:** protocol entropy for absolute-correctness claims is maxed.
Further pre-contact “perfect Done” essays are theater. Remaining progress:
deploy, measure TTD, let embodied traffic write the next regression.
